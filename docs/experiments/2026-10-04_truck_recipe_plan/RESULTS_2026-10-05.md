## What changed the numbers (all arms got the same search, P9)

* The recipe was the main factor: baseline ppo_gru 32.45 / 8.16 -> 41.56 / 13.59 (reference recipe: gamma 0.925-0.99, lambda 0.625, value-target
  EMA normalization, grad-norm 0.5, 8 minibatches, LayerNorm + [h, emb] heads, GRU-512). Anchors: `ref` (Dedieu) beat `moon` by 6-10 reward_pct
  for every arm; the best random point `r01` (64x64, lr 1.5e-3, 3x8, gamma 0.99) was best for Truck (42.7 on tuning seeds) and close for ppo_gru.
* S3 tuning means (2 seeds): Truck family mem+pa+hs 43.9 / 12.8, pa 43.2 / 12.6, mem 41.3 / 14.0, plain 42.7 / 11.8, hs-nocand 37.3, hs 33.5 (one
  seed 27); baseline family gru512 44.2 / 14.7, gru512+ln+skip 42.6 / 14.9, gru384+ln+skip 40.9, gru256+ln 40.1, gru256+ln+skip 40.0, gru256+skip 39.2.
  Differences inside a family are ~1-2 SE of 2-seed means: the variant ranking is mostly noise.
* Winner's curse is visible: Truck-improved scored 43.9 on tuning seeds 1000-1001 and 39.3 on the 10 final seeds; the baseline kept its level (44.2 -> 41.6).

## Caveats / things NOT done (recorded, not hidden)

* The 10 h profile dropped S0 (replication/determinism), S1 (death diagnostic) and `cnn_gru`; 2 tuning seeds, 4 random points. TASK-017's 39.19 for
  Truck remains unreplicated (the final Truck mean 39.29 is a different config and recipe).
* Truck at tf_gru r00 and r02 (48x96, 4 minibatches) failed with CUDA OOM on the 8 GiB card, also alone; excluded for Truck only (recorded as a failed
  run in MLflow, logs in `tune/failed_logs/`). The baseline did run those points, so the baseline had slightly more feasible points.
* Truck and baseline were tuned to different recipe points (Truck: r01, baseline: ref), as the selection rule dictates.
* The attribution control (`hs-nocand`, no candidate-action tokens) was within noise of `hs`, so a contribution of the candidate tokens is not shown.
* ppo_gru_ln (gru256+ln+skip) was the best baseline in S2; the S3 winner is gru512 (2.8M), i.e. part of the baseline gain is baseline size.
* No claim that Truck needs fewer parameters to reach a given accuracy is supported: Truck-improved is 0.47M vs 2.8M but not better in mean score.
